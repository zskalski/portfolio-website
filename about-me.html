About Me

I am a third-year Computer Science student at Florida State University with a 3.97 GPA and a strong interest in systems programming, low-level software, networking, and understanding how complex software systems work beneath the surface. I enjoy learning as much as I can about software development, especially in areas where programs, operating systems, networks, and hardware interact.

At Florida State University, I am involved in research through the Networked AI Systems Lab and serve as a Freshman Interest Group Leader for first-year Computer Science and Engineering students. I am also taking advanced coursework, including graduate-level classes in Advanced Operating Systems and Artificial Intelligence.

My career interests are focused on the defense and aerospace industries, particularly in areas such as missile defense, real-time systems, distributed systems, and mission-critical software.
